##Title: "Read Guide" button is not clickable — no action triggered on click

**Environment:
OS: Windows 11 
Browser: Chrome 135.0.7049.85 
Screen resolution: 2560X1440

**Steps to reproduce
1. Open the page: https://registry-staging.onrender.com/
2. Locate the "Read Guide" button.
3. Click on the button.
