## Bug Report #5

**Title:**  
Buttons on the creation form are not adjusted for mobile resolutions

**Date tested:** 14 April 2025

**Severity:** Medium  

**Priority:** Medium 

**Environment:**  
- OS: Windows 11, Android 14.0, iOS 18.3.2 - **the issue persists on all.**
- Browser: Chrome 135.0.7049.85, Firefox 137.0.1, Microsoft Edge 135.0.3179.73 - **The issue is resolution-independent and reproducible on any screen size.**
- Screen resolution: Less than 550px width (e.g., 430x932)
- Device: Windows laptop connected to a 27-inch monitor (only monitor screen being used), OnePlus 10T, iPhone 14Pro - **the issue persists on all.**

**Steps to Reproduce:**  
1. Open the page: https://registry-staging.onrender.com/.
2. Click on the button "Log In"
3. Authorize via GitHub.
4. Click on the button "Create Blueprint".
5. In the first form of creation, in the field "Upload test .eml," upload the file "residency.eml".
6. Locate the buttons "+ View sample .eml", "Save as Draft", "Next" at the bottom of the form.

**Expected Result:**  
- The buttons "+ View sample .eml," "Save as Draft," and "Next" are fully visible without the need to scroll horizontally.
- The buttons should be properly adjusted for mobile screen sizes and displayed within the viewport without requiring side scrolling.
  
**Actual Result:**  
- The buttons are not adjusted for mobile screen sizes and are not fully visible without horizontal scrolling.
- The "Next" button is positioned outside the viewport and requires side scrolling to be accessed.
