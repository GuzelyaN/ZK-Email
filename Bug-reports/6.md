## Bug Report #6

**Title:**  
"Draft" item is not included in the "Filter & Sort" dropdown list

**Date tested:** 14 April 2025

**Severity:** Medium  

**Priority:** Medium 

**Environment:**  
- OS: Windows 11, Android 14.0, iOS 18.3.2 - **the issue persists on all.**
- Browser: Chrome 135.0.7049.85, Firefox 137.0.1, Microsoft Edge 135.0.3179.73 - **The issue is resolution-independent and reproducible on any screen size.**
- Screen resolution: 2560x1440, 1024x1366, 430X932 - **the issue persists on all.**
- Device: Windows laptop connected to a 27-inch monitor (only monitor screen being used), OnePlus 10T, iPhone 14Pro - **the issue persists on all.**

**Steps to Reproduce:**  
1. Open the page: https://registry-staging.onrender.com/.
2. Click on the "Filter & Sort" Button

**Expected Result:**  
- The item "Draft" is present in the list and available for selection.
    
**Actual Result:**  
- The item "Draft" is missing from the list and not available for selection.
