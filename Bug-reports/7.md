## Bug Report #7

**Title:**  
View and Verify’ step is not displayed after completing Blueprint creation

**Date tested:** 15 April 2025

**Severity:** Medium  

**Priority:** Medium 

**Environment:**  
- OS: Windows 11, Android 14.0, iOS 18.3.2 - **the issue persists on all.**
- Browser: Chrome 135.0.7049.85, Firefox 137.0.1, Microsoft Edge 135.0.3179.73 - **The issue is resolution-independent and reproducible on any screen size.**
- Screen resolution: 2560x1440, 1024x1366, 430X932 - the issue persists on all.
- Device: Windows laptop connected to a 27-inch monitor (only monitor screen being used), iPhone 14Pro - **the issue persists on all.**

**Steps to Reproduce:**  
1. Open the page: https://registry-staging.onrender.com/.
2. Click on the button "Log In"
3. Authorize via GitHub.
4. Click on the button "Create Blueprint".
5. Fill in all the required fields on the "Connect emails" step.
6. On the "Select emails" step, select an email and click on the "Remote Proving" field.
   
**Expected Result:**  
  "View and verify" step is displayed
  
**Actual Result:**  
The form remains on the "Select emails" step
